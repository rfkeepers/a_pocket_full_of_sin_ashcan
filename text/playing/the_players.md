## Your agendas

### 1. Give import to action

Psychic pockets appear with a motley collection of shapes and seeming: brutally mundane and dreamily surreal, evasively calm and meticulously surprising. In the mire of it all you'll need to start with the most simple question, _what even matters here?_

_"This is all a brief fantasia,"_ you might say. _"I'm not culpable for anything I do. Should I accrue a debt, no collector will come knocking at my door. No officer will jail me if I commit a crime. Beholden to no one and no thing, shouldn't I do as I please?"_

Well, yes- and we'll return to the topic below- you should do as you please. It comes to this, _what you do_ is a smaller concern than _what it means_ when you do it. The act cannot amount to nothing.

Here is your compass: regard every matter as one of consequence, act as if the results are irrevocable.

### 2. Apply yourself

Your sojourn to each pocket is brief, precious, and precarious.

You are a storm. A fire. A rampage. A church. The succor of god. 

Set things in motion. Burn it down. Build it up.

### 3. Seek new experience

Fall in love. Get in fights. Be mean. Be gentle. Give yourself the opportunity to open up. Give yourself the opportunity to listen when someone else opens themselves to you.
