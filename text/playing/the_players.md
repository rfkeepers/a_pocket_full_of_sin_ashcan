# The Players
<p class="subtext">Everyone at the table who isn't the Mistress of Chimeras.</p>

This game hands its players a conventional roleplay experience; in the majority of cases, at least. They have their own characters to inhabit and explore and, unless told otherwise, those characters form the lens through which each player interacts in the conversation and, thusly, the world. The MC meanwhile conducts the ceremony of play and acts on behalf of all the world surrounding the players' characters.

Despite that tradition, players in this game are encouraged (expected, even!) to take equal claim in authorial topics such as, "What draws our interest?", "Why do we care about the consequences?", and even "What is this all about?". Different phases of the game will make you tangle with this goal in different ways, but the same premise holds throughout: you are all here to create the story together, not to receive it from one spinner of yarns alone.

## Your pocket agendas

These rules should guide your play within the Psychic Pockets, where you'll be acting as your character.

### 1. Give import to action

Psychic pockets appear in a motley collection of shapes and seeming: brutally mundane and dreamily surreal, evasively calm and meticulously surprising. In the mire of it all you'll need to start with the most simple question, _what even matters here?_

_"This is all a brief fantasia,"_ you might say. _"I'm not culpable for anything I do. Should I accrue a debt, no collector will come knocking at my door. No officer will jail me if I commit a crime. Beholden to no one and no thing, shouldn't I do as I please?"_

Well, yes- and we'll return to the topic below- you should do as you please. It comes to this, _what you do_ is a smaller concern than _what it means_ when you do it. The act cannot amount to nothing.

Here is your compass: regard every matter as one of consequence, act as if the results are irrevocable.

### 2. Apply yourself

Your sojourn to each pocket is precarious, precious, and brief. You have no time to shilly-shally or give yourself over to philosophical musings. Does a sprinter deliberate the starting gun? Does a spark pause to ponder the tinder?

There are no locks for which you must first find a key. No necessary clues to discover and no elaborate plans to detail and execute. Not that you cannot- or should not- rummage for the secrets, crowbar the locks, or trace a cascade of events. Those, too, are ways to apply yourself. But they are not prerequisite. When all is said and done your options are only and ever: what are you changing, how are you changing it?

So set things in motion. Burn places down. Build them up. Leave permanent fixtures and stitches in your wake.

### 3. Seek new experience

This is an occasion for you to encounter strange and rarefied experiences. Yes, you, reading this passage. Your character as well; I do mean both of you, though your reasoning in each moment may differ from your counterpart's. You are both equally important.

Fall in love. Make attachments. Get in fights. Be cruel, or gentle, or accepting of another's inclination to do same to you. Give yourself a chance to open up. Someone may open themselves to you; give yourself the opportunity to listen. What then? Tribulation and baptism and merriment and more and more and more.

Talk this over with your fellow players. Tell them what you'd like to try. Ask about their own lack and wants. Turn your pursuits to those highlights.
