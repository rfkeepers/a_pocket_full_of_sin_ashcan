# Instability

Any foreign intrusion into a psychic pocket will inevitably destabilize its foundation, tugging against the fabric of that universe until it begins to unravel.  The result is not only devastating to the owner of that pocket, it also serves to make the world around the players increasingly inhospitable.

What does that mean, exactly? How does instability manifest? That depends: every person holds dear to a different set of stabilizers in their life. The familiarity of well known places, of comforting faces, and of cherished items can all stake into place a center around which all the rest revolves.

Opposite that center are the things that fall out of order first. The passive details, unimportant presences, and their barely-maintained securities. How these details get evoked is up to the table- particularly (though not strictly) the Mistress of Chimeras. Mechanically, you'll only track the severity of the situation.  The rest is up to you.

## Tracking Instability

Instability gets tracked with an "instability die".  This die both tracks your current instability (the count of sides on the die and current pips) and also the acts the part of the pocket, the antagonist to your rolls (see: [rolling](/text/mechanics/rolling.md)).

When entering a pocket, instability always begins at `1 of d4` and counts upwards or downwards as mechanics demand. When the count outgrows the sides on the die (ex: `5 of d4`), then your instability upgrades to the next die, in the following order:

<dl>
  <dt>d4</dt>
  <dd>The pocket is stable and calm.</dd>
  <dt>d6</dt>
  <dd>The pocket trembles: oddities begin to appear, the things hidden deep within the unconscious poke and scratch at the weakening boundary. Merely suggestion; your nose prickles at the reek of the wrong, but you'll never find its source.</dd>
  <dt>d8</dt>
  <dd>All of reality begins to shift, warp, and roil. Continuity falters. Figments of the deep unconscious pry apart the seams and slip into the conscious space.</dd>
  <dt>d10</dt>
  <dd>Upheavals, chaos, and confusion.  The stitching of reality hangs loose.  The characters' remaining time here is untenable. The pocket itself is untenable.</dd>
</dl>

If the players reach `11 of d10` the pocket falters and all visitors get ejected. That's the end of that. They will not be permitted to return.
