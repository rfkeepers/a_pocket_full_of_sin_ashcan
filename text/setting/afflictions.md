# Afflictions

Although it may be incorrect to claim that the imprint of a person is defined by their collective stigmas, it stands without want of further proof that should a light be shined upon a soul exposed then the shadow thrown into relief would reveal, by trace of its silhouette, all the repugnant markings of iniquity: fissures of pain, carbuncles that weep with susurrations of guilt, imps shrieking and clutching with their bony little fingers at tongue and wrist and groin, and all clouded in the gloomy fogs of convenient forgetfulness.

Humans attract all manner of psycheal malignancies. Each of these troubles introduces some new **Affliction** to the pocket. They can be difficult to describe in specific, these afflictions, so eclectic and manifold are their appearances. One fundamental truth ties them all together: they are each the manifestation of some psychosocial harm experienced by a person.

Internalized blights are common enough: traumas, the conscience of broken taboos, wishes unfulfilled and failures unforgiven. There are, as well, shall we say, infections. Should one sign a contract with the devil, for example, that would certainly open a psychic door and permit some nasty creature to slip inside.

The rules for playing out afflictions are simple and lenient. Characters take on afflictions when a rule says they must (or, if so fancied, whenever they feel like it). What that means- the effects and affects, its evocation how it presents in play- are entirely for the table to decide. Wear these problems on your sleeve, wax sentimental about the torments they inflict, say how they leap out from your mind to infect pockets with babbling lunacy, or, don't at all. It's also perfectly all right to let an affliction sit quietly on your character sheet, as a mere note to yourself.

The MC may, whenever they have the opportunity to make a play, invoke any characters' afflictions. The consequences of such an invocation is up to their dreadful imagination: there are no strict rules here, either. This is the ultimate consequence of living with an affliction. One cannot predict when the thing might snarl and snap.
