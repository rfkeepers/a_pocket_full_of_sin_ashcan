## Plays

#### I'll be right behind you!

When you **linger behind and let everyone else walk off** while they assume you've tagged along with them, roll. On 2, pick two. On 1, pick one. On a miss, pick one anyway, and the MC will tell you how it goes bad.

- _I wander until I'm lost, where do I end up?_
- _I poke around to see what there is to find, what's here?_
- <i>Someone else finds me, it's</i> <span class="blank"></span>.
- <i>They left</i> <span class="blank"></span> <i>here, right out in the open. How foolish.</i>
- _I have a moment to relax, and am `Grounded`_.

#### These alleys hear all your whispered secrets

When you **sink into the background**, slipping behind the lights into the liminal space between the props and the backdrop, roll. On 2, pick two. On 1, pick one. On a miss, maybe the MC lets you pick one anyway, but no matter: the background is all impossible angles and malconnected sequence, and in short order you've gotten lost in it - you're trapped in this space until another character finds you and **Draws You Out**.

- _I overhear something to pique my interest; who is talking, what do they say?_
- _I'm safe, protected by whatever is in front of me, and cannot be reached._
- _I see someone from a new angle and learn of their secret heart and nature. Who?_
- _From this vantage I can see how this was all set up: what's real, what's fake, and what's facsimile._
- _Secretly, I witness something terribly important happen; what? And what does it portend?_

#### Little more than a shadow

When you **reduce your body to a shadow**, flattening down until you become a mere projection of some other nearby person, your feet attached to theirs: roll. On 2, choose 2 or 3. On 1, choose 1 or 2. On a miss you get carried away, more that person's shadow than your own self. Ask or tell the MC what happens.

- _I can move their limbs as if they were my own._
- _We keep good company with each other company, and stave off our worries._
- _I trust them to take me exactly where I want to go._
- _I can mimic their every move, no one could possibly find me._
- _I'm not actually attached, and can walk away whenever I want._
- _I'm comfortable, and can stay this way as long as I like._

## Burdens
<br>
<dl class="selectable">
  <dt>Head</dt>
  <dd>
  Whomever peers into your eyes sees: vacancy, an endless ocean, hollow nothing, mist, silver, opaline orbs, roiling clouds.

  You cannot <strong>Catch Their Eye</strong>. Nor can anyone Catch Your Eye unless you let them see you.
  </dd>
  <dt>Heart</dt>
  <dd>
  Your movements and expressions look: adrift, withered, asleep, transparent, numbed, buried, diaphanous, entangled.

  You cannot <strong>Lay Bare Your Heart</strong>. Nor can anyone Lay Bare Their Heart to you unless you let them understand you.
  </dd>
  <dt>Hands</dt>
  <dd>
  The touch of your skin feels: icy, incorporeal, fragile, leaden, papery, feverish, wanting.

  You cannot <strong>Barge In</strong> on anyone. Likewise, none can Barge In on you directly- unless you let them touch you- though they may treat you part of the audience by accident.
  </dd>
  <dt>Tongue</dt>
  <dd>
  Your voice sounds: wispy, ashen, formless, sedate, distant, airy, dreamlike, lost, sonorous.

  You cannot <strong>Draw Them Out</strong>. Nor can anyone Draw You Out unless you let them speak to you.
  </dd>
  <dt>Bones</dt>
  <dd>
  Whomever holds you feels a body that is: weightless, gossamer, insubstantial, incomplete, tremulous, decaying, crystalline.

  You cannot start a <strong>Quarrel or Fracas</strong>. Likewise, when you would get drawn into an altercation, you may forego any goal in the situation to instead sit still and quiet while the ordeal passes you over.
  </dd>
</dl>
<br>

### Your burdens overwhelm you: Disappear, apparition

**When you would mark a `sixth` burden,** don't. Your burdens overwhelm you. Erase three burdens immediately.

> sinkhole; you turn into nothing, and suck everything in with you

You become an apparition until you leave this pocket- as visible and corporeal as the silver morning mist. Everyone perceives you as a ghost or phantasm. You may turn invisible at whim and can pass through any solid object. In fact, under normal circumstances you cannot touch anything at all. Anytime you wish to physically interact with the world, first increase instability by `2`.
