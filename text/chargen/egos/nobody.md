## Moves

#### I'll be right behind you!

When you **linger behind and let everyone else walk off** while they assume you've tagged along behind them, roll. On 2, pick two. On 1, pick one. On a miss, pick one anyway, and the MC will tell you how it goes bad.

- _I wander until I'm lost, where do I end up?_
- _I poke around to see what there is to find, what's here?_
- <i>Someone else finds me, it's</i> <span class="blank"></span>.
- <i>They left</i> <span class="blank"></span> <i>behind, right out in the open. How foolish.</i>
- _I have a moment to relax, and am `Grounded`_.

#### These alleys hear all your whispered secrets

When you **sink into the background**, slipping behind the lights into the liminal space between the props and the backdrop, roll. On 2, pick two. On 1, pick one. On a miss, maybe the MC lets you pick one anyway, but no matter: the background is all impossible angles and malconnected sequence, and in short order you've gotten lost in it.

You're trapped creeping around this space until another character finds you and **Draws You Out**.

- _I overhear something to pique my interest; who is talking, what do they say?_
- _I'm safe, protected by whatever is in front of me, and cannot be reached._
- _I see someone from a new angle and learn of their secret heart and nature. Who?_
- _From this vantage I can see how this was all set up: what's real, what's fake, and what's facsimile._
- _Secretly, I witness something terribly important happen; what? And what does it portend?_

#### Give them an inch

When you **drop your defenses**, someone or something takes notice and moves in on you; roll. On a hit, you get to say who or what you've drawn in. On 2, you're aware and ready to meet them when they appear. On a miss, tell the MC what you hoped would happen, then prepare for the worst.

### Overburdened: Apparition

**When you would mark a `sixth` burden,** don't. Your burdens overwhelm you. Erase three burdens immediately.

> sinkhole; you turn into nothing, and suck everything in with you

You become an apparition until you leave this pocket- as visible and corporeal as the silver morning mist. Everyone perceives you as a ghost or phantasm. You may turn invisible at whim and can pass through any solid object. In fact, under normal circumstances you cannot touch anything at all. Anytime you wish to physically interact with the world, first increase instability by `2`.
