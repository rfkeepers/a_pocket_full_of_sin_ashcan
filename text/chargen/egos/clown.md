## Plays
<p class="subtext">Pick 1 to start.</p>

####  Bouffon

**When you create a character sheet template,** roll.  On 2, the sheet is complete and ready for you to start working on individual playbooks. On 1, you could play ahead, but you'll spend a lot of time fixing up all the little things when you inevitably change your mind.  On a miss, there's still so much to be done.

####  Send in the clowns

When you **turn a situation into an absurd spectacle,** roll.  On any hit, choose one or two from below. On 2, pick another, but only the first part. On a miss: don't they love farce? Your fault, you fear. You thought that they'd want what you want. Sorry, my dear, they do not. The MC may still let you pick one, but they'll tell you how your interruption ruins the mood for everyone.

- Take a step toward Grounded as you find firm footing for your antics; another character of your choosing steps toward Adrift as you fling them into mid-air.
- Take a step toward Adrift as you prance and somersault around, unable to be caught; another character of your choosing takes a step toward Grounded, they can't move with you dancing about.
- Where are the clowns? They're already here! A whole troupe of performers appears to put on a show; increase instability by 2. They're raucous, disorganized, and want to start their own chaos more than they mean to help you out; increase instability by an additional 2.
- Isn't this bliss? Pick a character, they can't help but approve of your clowning- say how they're bound to you. The MC picks another character who thinks your timing is terribly off, and binds them to you.
- You chase all the bothersome busybodies in the background out of the scene, leaving only the most important characters behind. Remove yourself as well; it'll be a while before you make it back. Perhaps you get lost somewhere in the background.
