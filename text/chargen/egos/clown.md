## Plays

####  Bouffon

When you **lay bare the truths** which people around you would rather avoid or keep hidden, roll. On any hit you leave no room for anyone to retaliate or censor you without others turning against them. On 2, pick two. On 1, pick one. On a miss, you've stepped over a line and all the ire has turned to you instead of them. Prepare for the worst.

####  Send in the clowns

When you **turn a situation into an absurd spectacle,** roll.  On any hit, choose one or two from below. On 2, pick another, but only the first part. On a miss: don't they love farce? Your fault, you fear. You thought that they'd want what you want. Sorry, my dear, they do not. The MC may still let you pick one, then they'll tell how your interruption ruins the mood for everyone.

- You find firm footing for your antics, take a step toward Grounded. Fling a character of your choosing into mid-air, they step towards Adrift.
- You prance and somersault around, unable to be caught; step towards Adrift. A character of your choosing is locked into place, unable to move with you dancing about; they step towards Grounded.
- Where are the clowns? They're already here! A whole troupe of performers appears to aid in the show; increase instability by 2. They're raucous, disorganized, and want to start their own chaos more than they mean to help you out; increase instability by an additional 2.
- Isn't this bliss? Pick a character, they can't help but enjoy your clowning- bind them to you. The MC picks another character who thinks your timing is terribly off, and binds you to them.
- You chase all the bothersome busybodies in the background out of the scene, leaving only the most important characters behind. Remove yourself as well; it'll be a while before you make it back. Perhaps you get lost somewhere in the background.
