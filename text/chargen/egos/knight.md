## Plays

####  Valorous armaments

You enter every pocket equipped with your Sword and Shield.

Your shield can block any problem that comes your way (attacks, dangers, accusations, seductions, etc), just say, **"No `X` may ever harm me!"**, and increase instability by `2`.

Your sword can cut down any obstacle that stands in your way (foes, fiends, walls, illusions, lies, fear, etc), just say, **"No `X` can bar my way!"**, and increase instability by `3`.

#### Herald your arrival

When you **proclaim your arrival with fanfare and pageantry**, roll; all the figments and afflictions in this place will hail your status and station. On 2, pick three. On 1 pick one, then the MC will also pick one. On a miss the MC picks one or two for you and, in addition, may say how that station is troubled or problematic. In all cases, ask or tell the MC what customs must be observed while you're here. Should you break any of them then your status will become irrecoverably tarnished.

- O' courteous traveler, we have safe haven for thee.
- O' hero, let us celebrate your appearance.
- O' regent, lead us in this time of need.
- O' shepherd, disabuse us of this veil.
- O' envoy, our attendant will address your needs.
- O' conqueror, please hear our plea, and spare us.
- O' champion, we hear your challenge, and accept.
- O' fool, no one here knows your name.
- Any other choice, and the need to abide local customs, extends to all those with you.

#### Heroic resolve

When you **set yourself to the completion of a trial** which no ordinary person could accomplish, then maybe, just maybe, you can do it; roll. On a 2, though nearly bowed, you do it. Ask the MC 1 of the following. On 1, it takes every bit of you, through tears and blood, being to persevere, ask 2.
- _I can almost do it, just one more set of hands would surely tip the scales._ Who offers their help?
- _I will endure any pain._ What parts of me are broken by the end?
- _I will take as much time, and as many attempts, as it requires._ How long am I at it? Who loses faith in me, turning away from the ordeal, before I succeed?
- _It certainly seems impossible._ How much does instability increase?

On a miss, choose 1 of the following.
- _Commitment means naught, the task is truly impossible._ Ask one from above anyway- the cost of your efforts- before you accept defeat.
- _I’m willing to ruin myself._ And, so, you do it. Ask the MC what price you have to pay.

### Your burdens overwhelm you: Here be dragons

**When you would mark a sixth burden,** don't. Your burdens overwhelm you. Immediately erase 3 of them.

_Chivalry in times of peace is fine and good for all. Yet what value brings a bravely knight who finds no lord at trouble, no maiden's plaintive call? All the more, a mighty quest would fit the best, a dragon fearsome full! Bring out the beast! I have mine sword, and oaths to which I'm swore. Bring on the beast! I'll have my dues, and title proven withal!_

Some affliction or monstrosity within the pocket grows to tremendous size. From this moment on it will rampage through the pocket: reducing buildings to rubble, instilling terror with its very nearness, making unreasonable demands for sacrifice, covetously protecting places of great value, and such other ill deeds.

Should you fail to slay it before you leave the pocket, it will assuredly destroy that person's mind. But should someone else slay it in your stead then your claim to a position of honorable knighthood will be challenged, and you'll take a new affliction for your weakness.
