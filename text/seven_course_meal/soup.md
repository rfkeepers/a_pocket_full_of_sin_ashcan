# Soup

The MC begins by revealing the following:

<dl>
  <dt>How tight is your leash?</dt>
  <dd>How much time and opportunity do you have to go sniffing around? Do they leave you under a watchful eye, set a tail on your heels, or let you loose to cause trouble?</dd>
</dl>

---

Each player may ask one from this list. The same question may be asked multiple times.
- _I'll bet `X` would let a secret slip. What do they tell me? Why are they unreliable?_
- _I steal away to peek at `X`. What scrap or fragment do I find?_
- _I corner `X` in private. What hint do they unintentionally drop?_
- _I have have evidence contradicting `X`. What do I have, and what does it prove?_
- _`X` and I have a candid discussion. What do I learn about their desires?_
- _`X` blocks me from going any farther. What are they protecting?_
- _I have a theory about all of this. Who hears me out? Who rebukes me?_
