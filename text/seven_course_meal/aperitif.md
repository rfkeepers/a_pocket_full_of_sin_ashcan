# Aperitif

Each player selects the physic which unfastens their mind and transports them into the Pocket..

Liquor is only partially a requirement: the psychotropic additives necessitate some alcohol in the solution, else they flush through the body without taking much effect. Your predecessors made due with lousy ale and fermented sugars. Mores the worse for them. A refined flavor helps the medicine go down.

Besides, what is a nice dinner without a drink ahead of the main course?

  Start the pocket feeling <code>Adrift</code>. You can reduce the cost of one  Instability play by up to <code>2</code>.

  The first time you **Quarrel and Fracas**, don't roll. Treat the result as a `2` instead.</dd>
