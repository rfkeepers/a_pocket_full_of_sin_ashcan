# Moves in Review

### Simple Sleight of Mind

You can produce playful illusions at whim, just say, **"I told you that I'm a wizard"**, then say what how your illusion looks. Increase instability by `1` to conjure a foolish, fleeting, obviously false display. Increase instability further to make the illusion enduring (`+1`), believable (`+2`), or substantial (`+2`).

### At Home in the Elements

Instability moves increase instability by 1 fewer points when you use them (you cannot combine this effect to reduce instability).
