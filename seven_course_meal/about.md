# About the Seven-Course Meal

The seven course meal guides play through a structured set of scenes wherein the MC and Players get introduced to their employers, learn the superficials of the next job, investigate clues and verify details, and otherwise prepare for the journey into psychic pocket.

Your employers all invariably share a couple things in common: they want the best for their loved one; they think of you as a tool and a means to an end- not as a friend or benefactor; they know there are skeletons in the closet, they want those secrets to stay hidden, and the chance that you might reveal any of their sordid history makes you as dangerous as you are necessary.

Think of this phase as a cinematic cross-cut: the characters already sit at the dinner table to partake in the ritual which sends them into the psychic pocket. With each new plate we reveal them, in a past moment, catching uncertain glances, peeking through the keyholes of locked doors, and wheedling clues out of their employers' suspicious associates.

The meal phase separates into seven parts, always in the same order. The first five give the players a specific set of questions to ask and bonuses to choose. The sixth part moves play into the next phase and the players enter the Psychic Pocket for the remainder of the game. Afterward, the party returns to the meal and finishes the job.
