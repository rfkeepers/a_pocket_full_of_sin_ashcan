# Palate Cleanser

Before asking the next questions the players may take a few minutes to study and debate the knowledge they've received. Some dispute is expected. Nothing here has been decided. They may not, under any circumstance, ask the MC for opinions, insights, or answers. The MC is allowed correct any mistakes, but may not invent any answers until the following questions are asked.

---

These questions are all directed at your employer. Each player picks one from the list.
- _You didn't tell us about `X`._
- _I think you're lying about `X`. Will you come clean with the truth?_
- _We found out about `X`. Why hide that from us?_
- _`X` wants to know what we know. Why shouldn't we give them what they want?_
- _I think `X` is involved. What do you think?_
- _If we can't save this person, what would you have us do?_
