# Palate Cleanser

Before asking the next questions the players may take a few minutes to study and debate the knowledge they've received. Some dispute is expected. Nothing here has been decided.

Players may not ask the MC for opinions, insights, or hints. The MC is allowed correct mistakes, but may not provide answers until the players get around to picking questions.

---

These questions are all directed at your employer. Each player picks one from the list.
- _I still don't understand `X`. Can you help me?_
- _We found out about `X`. Why hide that from us?_
- _I think you're lying about `X`. Will you come clean with the truth?_
- _I think `X` is involved somehow. What do you think?_
- _`X` wants us to `Y`. Why shouldn't we give them what they want?_
- _If we can't save this person, what do you want us to do?_
