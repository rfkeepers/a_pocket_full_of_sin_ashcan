# Hors D'oeuvre

The MC begins by revealing the following:

<dl>
  <dt>Whom you're here to save</dt>
  <dd>What condition they're in, and what your employer wants you to do for them.</dd>
  <dt>Terms and conditions</dt>
  <dd>Any rules and requirements they expect you to follow through this endeavor.</dd>
</dl>

---

Each player may ask one from this list.
- _What makes them renowned or notorious?_
- _How did they like to spend their time each day?_
- _What was their life's goal? What did they actually achieve?_
- _Which person was most important in their life?_
- _Who were their bitter enemies and rivals?_
- _What does your employer think of them?_
