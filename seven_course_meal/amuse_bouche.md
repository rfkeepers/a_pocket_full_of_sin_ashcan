# Amuse-Bouche

The MC begins by revealing the following:

<dl>
  <dt>The employer</dt>
  <dd>Who they are and, if it's not obvious, whom they represent.</dd>
  <dt>The location</dt>
  <dd>Where this is all taking place.</dd>
  <dt>The contract</dt>
  <dd>What they expect you to do, and how much you'll get paid at the end.</dd>
</dl>

---

Each player may ask one from this list. The same question may be asked multiple times.
- _What seems amiss about our employer?_
- _What rumor or yarn precedes your employer?_
- _What do I notice about how they treat us or act around us?_
- _What topics do they ignore, avoid, or skirt around?_
- _Whom do I catch peeping as we arrive?_
- _Which of us upsets them first?_
- _Who is present or absent from the table, contrary to expectations?_
