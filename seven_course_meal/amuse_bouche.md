# Amuse-Bouche

The MC will always begin by explaining the following:
- Who the employer is (often themselves).
- Whom they represent.
- What the characters are here to do (if it's not obvious).

Each player may ask one of the following. The same question may be asked multiple times.
- _What seems amiss about the employer?_
- _What do I notice about how they treat us or act around us?_
- _What topics do they ignore, avoid, or skirt around?_
- _Whom do I catch peeping as we arrive?_
- _Which of us upsets them first?_
- _As we sit down to eat, whom at the table did we not expect?_
