# Instability Moves

Instability moves are used to tamper with the very integrity of the pocket.  They never get rolled; instead, they require payment in the form of instability. Gain enough instability and you'll destabilize the whole pocket, making the trip more difficult for all the players.

## Set the Mood

You can twist the emotion of any situation to better suit your mood, just say, "this isn't the feeling we need right now".  Increase instability by 1 to introduce a small, ambient effect of some kind: the sound of music, a gentle warmth or coolness, sudden laughter from a nearby figment.  Increase it by 3 to completely alter some part of the setting: a significant change in weather or time of day, appearance or dispersal of a crowd.

## Call For Help

You can make a figment or character appear wherever you're at, just say, "I could use a little help right now", then say who you need help from.  Increase instability by 1 if it makes sense that they could be there right now.  Increase it by 3 to get them there regardless of the circumstances.

## Have What You need

You can pull any item you need from the surrounding scenery, just say, "an \<item\> has to be around here somewhere".  Increase instability by 1 if that sort of thing matches your surroundings.  Increase it by 3 if the item would be out of place or unrealistic.

## Reveal a Secret

You can compel the pocket to reveal its secrets, yearnings, or tamperings, just say, "I can feel something here just beyond my reach", then tell the MC what kind of thing you're looking for. Increase instability by 1 and the MC will give you a breadcrumb to follow. Increase it by 3 and they'll lay the secret out plainly for all to see.
