# Instability Moves

Instability moves can tamper with the present truths of pocket reality. They never get rolled; instead, they require payment: every use increases the group's instability. The more obviously you contort reality to meet your goals, the more it costs. Gain enough instability and you'll destabilize the whole pocket, making the trip more difficult for everyone.

---
### Set the Stage

You can reframe any situation to better suit the mood you need, just say, **"this isn't the feeling we want right now"**.  Increase instability by `1` to introduce a small, ambient effect of some kind: the sound of music, a gentle warmth or coolness, sudden laughter from a nearby figment.  Increase it by `3` to completely alter some part of the scene, like a significant change in weather or time of day, appearance or dispersal of a crowd, or a sudden flood of water or outbreak of fire.

<div class="burden">

You may not set the stage as normal.  Instead, increase instability by `2` and a sudden peal of sound, light, or feeling rushes through the scene.  Increase it by `4` to displace or smother the scene: replacing the air with water, erasing all light, or removing all effects of gravity.
</div>

---
### A Summons for Aid

You can make a figment or person already within the pocket available in the scene, wherever you're at, just say, **"I'm in a spot, here"**, then say who you need help from (or open the offer to anyone).  Increase instability by `1` if it makes sense that they could be there right now.  Increase it by `3` to get them there regardless of the circumstances.

<div class="burden">

Someone or something else arrives with them.  Ask or tell them MC who or what.
</div>

---
### That's Just the Ticket

You can pull any item you need from the surrounding scenery, just say, **"an `X` has to be around here somewhere"**.  Increase instability by `1` if that sort of thing matches your surroundings.  Increase it by `3` if the item would be out of place or unrealistic.

<div class="burden">

This item comes out of you, pulled into this pocket from somewhere deep inside your own. This realization always takes you by surprise.  Describe the lost memory the item recalls when you pick it up.
</div>

---
### Disinter a Secret

You can compel the pocket to reveal its secrets, yearnings, or tamperings, just say, **"I can feel something here, barely beyond my reach"**, then tell the MC what kind of thing you're looking for. If there's nothing special to reveal, or it should be plain to see, they'll tell you about it outright.  Otherwise increase instability by `1` and the MC will give you a breadcrumb to follow. Increase it by `3` and they'll lay the secret out plainly for all to see.

<div class="burden">

The pocket gives you nothing for free. Whisper to it a secret, yearning, or tampering of your own.  Only afterward will it reveal itself to you.
</div>

---
### Make a Solemn Promise

When you make a solemn promise to anyone in the pocket- whether they're a figure or a person- repeat to them what you've promised to do, then say, **"I swear to it"**.  Decrease instability by `2` and write your promise on a bit of paper so you don't forget.  The moment you break your promise, whether or not someone is around to see you do it, increase instability by `5`.

<div class="burden">

When you make a promise, decrease instability by `3` instead of `2`.  You cannot break your promises.  However, whomever you swore these oaths to could always release you from the obligation.
</div>
